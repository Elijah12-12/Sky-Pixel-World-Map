SKY PIXEL ALPHA 18 OVERLAY

1. Upload these two files beside your other map files:
   - sky-pixel-alpha-18.css
   - sky-pixel-alpha-18.js

2. Add this line with your other stylesheet links:
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="sky-pixel-alpha-18.css">

3. Add this line after the uNmINeD/OpenLayers map scripts and Sky Pixel main script:
   <script src="sky-pixel-alpha-18.js"></script>

4. To add or edit highlighted regions later, open sky-pixel-alpha-18.js and
   edit the ALPHA_18_AREAS array near the top of the file.

The overlay starts hidden. The opening announcement lets visitors show it,
and the Alpha 18 Areas toggle can turn it on or off at any time.
